What we take care of

Care for every fabric
you own, done properly.

Wash & Fold

Everyday clothes washed in fabric-appropriate cycles and returned neatly folded, drawer-ready.

  • Sorted by colour and fabric before washing
  • Mild, skin-friendly detergents
  • Turnaround: about 48 hours
Everyday wear · Tees · Nightsuits

Dry Cleaning

Solvent-based care for structured garments that must keep their shape, finish and colour.

  • Garments returned on hangers
  • Collar, cuff and lining attention
  • Turnaround: about 72 hours
Suits · Blazers · Formal shirts

Steam Ironing

Temperature-controlled steam pressing that leaves crisp creases without shine or scorch marks.

  • Heat matched to each fabric type
  • Crease-safe folding or hanging
  • Turnaround: about 24 hours
Shirts · Trousers · Uniforms

Stain Treatment

Individual stain assessment (tannin, oil, protein or ink), then targeted pre-treatment.

  • Stain identified before any wash
  • Fabric-safe spot removers
  • Turnaround: with your wash order
Curry · Coffee · Ink · Sweat

Delicate Fabric Care

Hand-wash programmes and protective mesh bags for silk, lace, chiffon and embroidered pieces.

  • Cold water, minimal agitation
  • Air-dried away from direct sun
  • Turnaround: about 72 hours
Silk · Lace · Chiffon · Embroidery

Express Laundry

Same-day wash & fold for last-minute needs: in by morning, back by evening.

  • Dedicated fast-track load
  • Pickup slots from 8 AM
  • Turnaround: same day, by 8 PM
Meetings · Travel · Emergencies
