How it works

Six stages between hamper and hanger.

Each stage activates as you scroll. Tap a title for the detail.

6 tracked stages 48 hr typical turnaround 0 shop visits needed

  1. 01 Book

    Pick your services and a time slot online. Booking takes under a minute and needs nothing but your address and phone number.

    Choose wash & fold, dry cleaning or any mix of services. Add special instructions: separate the gym wear, skip the softener, fold the shirts flat. The rider sees your notes before pickup.

  2. 02 Pickup

    A rider arrives in your chosen slot with reusable bags. Your items are counted at the door and photographed as a record.

    Everything goes into a labelled MeraDhobi bag. You get a digital count of each category (6 shirts, 3 trousers, 2 bedsheets), so nothing is ambiguous about what left your home.

  3. 03 Sort & Inspect

    Every garment is inspected, tagged and grouped by fabric and colour before anything touches water.

    Loose buttons, small tears or stubborn stains are photographed and reported to you before cleaning, with a recommendation: repair, stain treatment, or a gentler programme.

  4. 04 Clean

    Each load runs its own programme: temperature, detergent and cycle length matched to the fabric.

    Cottons take a warm wash; silks and wool get cold, low-agitation care; denim goes in cold and inside-out. Dry-cleanable items never see water; they go through solvent care instead.

  5. 05 Quality Check

    Fresh loads are steamed, pressed and inspected piece by piece against a finishing checklist.

    Collars and cuffs get a second pass, buttons are checked, and anything that misses the standard is re-treated before delivery, not after you complain.

  6. 06 Deliver

    Your clothes return folded, protected and on time, usually within 48 hours of pickup.

    Formals come back on hangers, everyday wear is folded drawer-ready, and everything is wrapped against dust and drizzle. Delivery slots are confirmed the evening before.

Behind the scenes

Small systems that make it dependable.

Tagging

Every order travels with a wash tag, so mixed loads never lose their identity between stages.

Slot discipline

Pickup and delivery windows are one hour wide, and the rider calls before arriving.

Gentle chemistry

pH-balanced detergents and fabric-safe solvents, dosed per load rather than by habit.
